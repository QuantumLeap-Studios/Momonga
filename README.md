**Momonga** is a 3D Printing all-in-one platform that a built-in dedicated slicer & GCode Editor, OctoPrint support, fillament tracker, and more!

---

# Adding a Printer

## Manual

Simply go to printers, click **+ New Preset**, configure the settings to match your printer, optionally change the connection (else if its using COM Ports change the serial port) and connect it by clicking **Create preset**. Once it's created, your printer will show up in the list and be ready to use whenever you need it.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/QuantumLeap-Studios/Momonga_Assets/refs/heads/main/optimized_connectprinter.gif" width=450>

## Automatic

Simply click the **Connect** button at the top right, click OctoPrint (or whatever you'll need), and fill out what it asks you to. This is the easiest way to connect a wireless printer, since it only takes a few quick steps and everything else gets set up for you.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/QuantumLeap-Studios/Momonga_Assets/refs/heads/main/optimized_connectwirelessprinter.gif" width=450>

# 3-Step Printing

## Slicing

All you have to do is import your model, click slice, then open it in the dedicated GCode Editor. Slicing only takes a few moments, and once it's done you can jump straight into the editor to look over the result.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/QuantumLeap-Studios/Momonga_Assets/refs/heads/main/optimized_slice.gif" width=450>

## GCode Editing

Once you slice your model and open the editor, simply make the changes you want and then Upload (optional but you can also also save it). You can edit the GCode however you like, and when you're happy with it you can send it straight to your printer or keep a copy for later.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/QuantumLeap-Studios/Momonga_Assets/refs/heads/main/optimized_edit.gif" width=450>

## Print

Simply find the exported gcode and **Print > Confirm Print**. Give everything one last look, then confirm and your printer will get started on your model.

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/QuantumLeap-Studios/Momonga_Assets/refs/heads/main/optimized_print.gif" width=450>
